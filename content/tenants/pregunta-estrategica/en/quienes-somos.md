# Who We Are

**Pregunta Estratégica** was born from the commitment of two lawyers from the Universidad Católica del Norte (UCN) to excellence and rigorous preparation for the bar exam.

We are **Estefanía Montalbán Pino** and **Camila Ogalde Fonck**. Drawing on our own experience and with a strategic approach, we decided to create a space where exam preparation is not an obstacle, but a clear, demanding, and supported process.

Together we developed a unique method that combines:

- 📚 Personalized classes and tutoring.
- 🎤 Litigation skills and strategic oral expression.
- 🔍 Mock examinations with in-depth feedback.
- 💻 Access to an exclusive platform and app, featuring a student planner and a legal database built around the Constitutional, Civil, Procedural, and Criminal Law syllabi.

We believe the bar exam is best faced with **active preparation**, **strategic analysis**, and **quality tools**. That is why we offer two formats designed for you:

- 🎯 **Hybrid Program** — classes + mock examinations.
- ⚡ **Exam Intensive** — mock examinations with feedback only.

Both options include our legal database with regularly updated study notes.

---

**Pregunta Estratégica** is more than a course: it is the guidance of two UCN lawyers who know the challenge and help you face it with strategy.
