# Terms and Conditions — Código Estratégico

**Last updated:** August 21, 2026

The **Código Estratégico** program by Pregunta Estratégica is designed to reward our students for recommending our interrogation programs while also offering a benefit to those who join through a recommendation.

By taking part in the program, the student accepts the following terms and conditions.

---

## 1. Who can take part

The program is open to students enrolled in any of the **Pregunta Estratégica** class and interrogation programs for the law degree exam who have a personal Código Estratégico.

Each student receives a unique code they may share with anyone interested in our degree exam programs.

---

## 2. Benefit for the new referred student

Anyone who signs up using a valid Código Estratégico receives:

**$5,000 off per month for 3 months** on their interrogation program.

The benefit amounts to a maximum discount of **$15,000 in total**, spread across three consecutive monthly payments.

The discount applies starting with the first monthly payment of the program purchased.

The discount is personal, non-transferable, and cannot be exchanged for cash.

---

## 3. Benefit for the referrer

For every new student who enrolls correctly using their Código Estratégico, the referrer receives:

**$5,000 off their next interrogation pack.**

Benefits stack according to the following scale:

- **1 referral:** $5,000 off.
- **2 referrals:** $10,000 off.
- **3 referrals:** $15,000 off.
- **4 referrals:** $20,000 off.

The stacked discount is capped at **$20,000 per accumulation period**.

---

## 4. Special benefit for a fifth referral

Upon reaching a **fifth valid referral within the month**, the student receives as a special benefit:

**1 additional free interrogation on their next interrogation pack**, subject to the conditions of the program in force.

This benefit replaces a fifth $5,000 discount and does not result in an additional $25,000 discount.

---

## 5. What counts as a valid referral?

For a referral to be valid, all of the following conditions must be met:

- The referred person must be a **new Pregunta Estratégica student** in the Degree Exam study program.
- They must purchase one of the available interrogation programs.
- They must enter the Código Estratégico at the time of enrollment.
- The purchase must be effectively completed and paid.
- Renewals, plan changes, or new purchases made by students who have previously taken part in the interrogation programs do not count as valid referrals.

---

## 6. Use of the Código Estratégico

The code is personal and may be shared freely with anyone interested in Pregunta Estratégica programs.

It may not be used to generate fictitious or duplicate sign-ups, or sign-ups made with false information.

Pregunta Estratégica may invalidate a referral when it determines that there has been fraudulent use or use contrary to these conditions.

---

## 7. How benefits are applied

Benefits obtained through **Código Estratégico**:

- Cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Cannot be transferred to third parties.
- Do not generate account credit.
- Cannot be combined with other promotions or discounts, unless Pregunta Estratégica expressly states otherwise.

Discounts owed to the referring student apply to their **next interrogation pack** once the referral has been validated.

---

## 8. Term

The **Código Estratégico** program remains in force for as long as Pregunta Estratégica keeps it active.

Pregunta Estratégica may modify, suspend, or end the program, giving participants timely notice.

Referrals made and validated before the program is modified or ended keep the benefits that apply under the conditions in force at the time of validation.

---

## 9. Acceptance

Taking part in the **Código Estratégico** program implies full acceptance of these Terms and Conditions.
