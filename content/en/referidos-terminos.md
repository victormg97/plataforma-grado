## 1. About the program

The {{APP_NAME}} referral program lets enrolled students share a personal code
with other students. When someone signs up using that code, both the person who
shared it and the person who used it may become eligible for benefits.

## 2. Eligibility

Anyone with an active {{APP_NAME}} account can take part. The code is personal,
non-transferable, and every use is recorded in the platform.

## 3. Benefits

Benefit amounts, duration, and caps are the ones displayed on the program page
inside the platform. {{APP_NAME}} may update them with prior notice to
participants.

## 4. Conditions of use

- The code must be entered at sign-up or at the time of purchase.
- Benefits cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Using your own code or creating duplicate accounts is not allowed.

## 5. Term and changes

{{APP_NAME}} may change or end the program at any time. Benefits already granted
are honored under the conditions in force when they were obtained.
